## Where things stand

- Project: *Removed Is Not Safe* — chlorine (free chlorine vs pre-formed chloramine) + ciprofloxacin in a coiled-tube contact chamber, then bisulfite dechlorination, like the San José–Santa Clara plant. Synopsys fair, ~March 2027.
- Research question (v2 plan, Nov 1): how do chlorine dose, contact time, chlorine form and the dechlorinating agent change the antibacterial activity left in ciprofloxacin-containing water, and can a calibrated kinetic model predict that activity well enough to choose safer chlorination settings?
- Gaps (reframed Nov 1): (1) activity after pre-formed chloramine treats cipro; (2) how much cipro comes back when N-chloro-cipro meets bisulfite, how fast versus the plant's dechlorination time, and whether activity rises — sulfur(IV) reduction of N-chloramines to the parent amine is already known in general (Bedner & MacCrehan 2006; Dodd 2005 noted it for thiosulfate + cipro), so the novelty is the amount, rate and activity effect, not the reaction itself; (3) blind-tested model-chosen settings.
