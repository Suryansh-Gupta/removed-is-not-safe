The instrument gate

Decide by Friday, Week 2 — 2 Oct 2026

The ciprofloxacin fluorimeter (~280 nm excitation, ~450 nm emission) is the largest single line item at 30 h, neither of you has built one, and it sits where a slip propagates through everything downstream. So it gets three off-ramps, checked in order.

Never cut the 12 map runs, the 12 search runs, the 6 confirmation runs, the 4 scavenger runs, or the in-silico study. Together those are the whole evidentiary base — everything else is supporting material.

Where slack lives

Four absorb weeks — 12, 13, 20 and 21 — run at 7.0–7.5 h each by design. That is 51 person-hours of headroom, about 1.1 h/week each.

The searches stay at 12 runs, three proposal rounds each. The 6 deleted grid runs went into the map, not into the searches, because the map is the primary outcome and the training set for the surrogate that carries the machine-learning claim.
