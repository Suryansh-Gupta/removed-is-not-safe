Sampling portsA 0.95 · B 1.9 · C 2.85 m
Dechlorinationby hand, in each vial
  • Coil: wind the tube tightly on the PVC pipe, turns touching, and hold it with zip ties. 1/4″ OD PTFE kinks below about a 44 mm bend radius, so don't use anything smaller than the 4″ pipe.
  • Ports A–C: cut the tube at each distance, push both ends into a union tee, and put a shut-off valve on the side branch. Draw samples from the valve with a syringe.
  • Pump connections (4): PTFE tube → John Guest female adapter → 1/16″ barb (wrap the thread in PTFE tape) → the pump's own 2 mm BPT tube.
  • Probes: handheld pens, no logging. Check the buffered feed jug with the pH pen at the start and end of each run and spot-check a Port A sample; use the ORP pen to confirm quenched vials have no chlorine left.
  • Quench by hand: make 0.30 g/L sodium bisulfite fresh each run day; put 100 µL in each labelled 2 mL vial (double it for 10 mg/L runs). Run 0.5 mL to waste from the port, collect 2 mL, squirt it into the vial, cap, shake, note the time. For plant-timing samples hold 5 min before filtering. Check one vial per run day reads 0.00 mg/L on the DPD kit. The outlet still contains chlorine: collect it in a waste jug with a spoonful of bisulfite.
  • Chlorine tests take 10 mL each, which is 10 minutes of flow at 1 mL/min. Collect the sample over that time, or check chlorine in a separate run at higher flow.
Check before you order: Amazon blocks automated reading of product pages, so these items were picked from their listing titles and prices are estimates. Confirm three things: the pump listing is the 24 V stepper version with 2 mm × 4 mm BPT tubing; the 1/16″ barb fits snugly in 2 mm ID tubing; and each John Guest fitting says 1/4″ OD.

Pumps and controller

Two pumps: feed and chlorine. At 1 mL/min a 50 mL/min pump runs at about 2% of full speed. A stepper motor handles that well; calibrate each pump by weighing 10 minutes of output (1 g = 1 mL).

Borrow, don't buy

  • Analytical balance, 0.1 mg (for cipro)
  • Volumetric flasks and micropipettes
  • HPLC-UV instrument and training (BioCurious, Waters 2695; Family Membership $200/month, a parent present at all times)
  • Fume hood for bleach stock (if the SRC requires it)
  • Incubator at 37 °C (E. coli K-12 is allowed at BioCurious)
  • Plate reader, or read the resazurin colour by eye
