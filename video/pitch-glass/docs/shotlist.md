# Liquid glass pitch: script and shots

One piece of glass carries the story. Every shot's words say what the glass is doing. Both shapes are composed natively, with
no monitor or desk. Timing is on the keynote cut's score (120 bpm, 1 beat = 0.5 s), so the music is unchanged.

Glass rules from the `liquid-glass-motion` skill (yordilorenzo/liquid-glass-skills, MIT; SwiftUI, so used as reference, not
code): glass materializes by ramping its lensing and never fades; bigger is thicker; shapes merge and split as true morphs;
never black behind glass; only something with momentum bounces (from `apple-motion-feel`, so only the falling drop does).
The shader is `lib/glass.js`.
