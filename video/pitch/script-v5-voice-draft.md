# Pitch video: narrated script draft (about 90 seconds)

Style reference: short, spoken fragments with long pauses and fast cuts, studied from the Sol launch film by @a1tkid. Only the rhythm is borrowed, not its words, footage or voice. Every spoken line also appears as a caption. Draft for Suryansh and Ritik to edit.

| # | Voice (... = pause) | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every pill has a second life. | Black screen, words only |
| 2 | Prescribed. ... Swallowed. ... Flushed. ... Treated. | One quick shot per word |
| 3 | At the plant, chlorine hits the antibiotic in under a second. | 3D molecule; chlorine strikes |
| 4 | And the chemical test says: mostly gone. | Drug meter collapses |
| 5 | Chlorine destroyed 72% of it. | 72% fills the screen |
| 6 | ... 70% of its power was still there. | Bacteria meter barely moves; music drops out |
| 7 | Removed ... isn't the same as safe. | Title |
| 8 | Resistant bacteria directly caused one point two seven million deaths in 2019. | Number scene |
| 9 | So we stopped asking if the drug is gone. ... We started asking if its power is. | Black screen, then the two tests |
| 10 | We designed a treatment plant ... that fits on a bench. | **Prototype: full reveal** |
| 11 | Two pumps. ... One coil. ... Thirty minutes. | Pumps, coil, a clock |
| 12 | Three ports, to watch the reaction as it happens. | Ports A, B, C light up |
| 13 | Then we test every sample twice. ... How much drug. ... How much power. | HPLC peak and bacteria plate |
| 14 | A chemistry model suggests the next settings to try. | Model loop |
| 15 | And before the lab runs, we post our predictions. ... In public. | Timestamped prediction |
| 16 | The goal: settings that still disinfect, and leave less behind. | Settings dials |
| 17 | Removed isn't the same as safe. ... We're going to measure the difference. | End card with names and [contact or link] |

Sources: 72% and 70% are Kennedy Neth et al. 2019, Fig. 2 (lab water, 120 minutes; small on-screen credit). 1.27 million is Murray et al. 2022, The Lancet. "Under a second" and the molecule's breakdown are Dodd et al. 2005.

The prototype line says "designed" because the reactor is not built yet. Change it to "built" once it is.
