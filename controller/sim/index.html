Driver current

After a crash, the Arduino's watchdog stops the pumps 5 s later. Pulling its USB cable stops them at once.

Too little current skips steps. The torque numbers are PLACEHOLDERS until the motor's are known, so this shows the behaviour, not where the real limit is.
